THE ARK FAMILY GUIDE

Practical Information
for Our Families.

Everything you need to support your child's daily journey at The Ark, from daily routines to settling-in advice and policies.

A Typical Day at The Ark

Time Activity & Focus Area Description
8:00 – 9:00 AM Early Arrival & Breakfast Gentle arrival, morning greetings, handwashing, and nutritious light breakfast.
9:00 – 11:30 AM Uninterrupted Montessori Work Cycle Independent work with sensorial apparatus, practical life activities, individual presentations.
11:30 – 12:00 PM Circle Time, Music & Phonics Group singing, movement, language games, rhyming, and cultural storytelling.
12:00 – 1:00 PM Organic Lunch & Social Grace Children set their own tables, serve food with care, practice polite table etiquette.
1:00 – 3:30 PM Garden Discovery & Creative Arts Free-flow outdoor play, botany, painting, water play, and quiet rest area.

Nurturing Independence Every Day

Children flourish when given structure, gentle expectations, and autonomy. From self-serving snack routines to putting on coats and shoes, our daily flow reinforces confidence and pride in accomplishment.

We work in close partnership with parents, providing regular observations, photos, and developmental updates.
