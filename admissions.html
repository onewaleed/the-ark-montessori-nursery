ENROLMENT & FUNDING

Welcoming New Children
& Supporting Families.

We aim to make our admissions journey clear, warm, and supportive. We proudly accept 15 and 30 hours Government Early Years Funded Entitlement.

Funded Childcare for 2 to 5 Year Olds

We offer flexible scheduling options to suit working parents. Whether using 15 hours universal entitlement or 30 hours extended working parent funding, our team helps streamline the voucher application process.

Tax-Free Childcare and student childcare grants are also gladly accepted.

Session Fees & Funding Structure

We offer flexible full-day and half-day sessions. 15-hour and 30-hour government funding codes for eligible 2, 3, and 4-year-olds can be applied across term-time or stretched funding models.

Session Type Hours Standard Fee (Non-Funded) With 15/30 Hours Funding
Morning Montessori Session 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM £42.00 / session Fully Funded / Free Entitlement
